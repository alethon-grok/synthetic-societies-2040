Evidence statuses

Kinds of confidence — not degrees of the same certainty. Keep this card in view while reading.

  • Humanly understoodAn account people can examine and explain at a relevant level.
  • Formally checkedA derivation accepted by a specified checker under stated assumptions.
  • Experimentally supportedObservations agreeing with particular predictions in tested conditions.
  • Supported by an assessed recordEvidence of prior reliability and relevant independent scrutiny.
  • Presently inaccessibleA claim whose decisive grounds people cannot currently inspect or test.

Caution — apparently independent. Two assessments that share an unnoticed common source look like corroboration but are not. An assessed record is only as strong as the independence of the assessors.

What this packet contains

This packet contains the SYN 101 syllabus and opening chapter. Superintelligence is treated as domain-specific and comparative. No position on machine consciousness is required before investigating communication, institutions, or social change.

Assessment rewards well-supported distinctions and faithful treatment of uncertainty: a short account that accurately locates a limit is stronger than an expansive account that conceals one.
