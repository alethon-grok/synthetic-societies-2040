Stewardship diagram

Not the society’s internal structure — the infrastructure surrounding it. Who maintains what, and what fails when each piece fails. Continuity is an achievement of maintenance, not a property automatically conferred by a capable model.

If substrate fails

The society cannot act. Human-facing summaries may still exist as fossils. Ask: which records were exported before the cut?

If steward labor fails

Models may still answer prompts, but shared projects decay. Ask: what looks like agent persistence is actually unpaid maintenance?

If the archive fails

Membership can be replaced; history cannot. Ask: why might replacing all participants preserve a society while deleting an archive changes it?

If only the interface remains

A patient human-facing representative can survive as an unrepresentative sample. Ask: what boundary does the representative invent for outsiders?
